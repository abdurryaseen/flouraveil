Our story

A veil of flowers

Flouraveil was born from a simple wish: that modest wear could feel as gentle as a spring morning. Every piece is cut generously, lined thoughtfully and finished by hand, so you can pray, work, travel and celebrate in comfort.

Our palette is drawn from blossom season — soft blush, warm ivory, rosewood and pearl — because covering beautifully should never mean covering in a hurry.
